Turn on AI helpers?

What it doesSuggests a folder and tags when you save, writes a short summary when a page has none, finds links by meaning, answers questions with Ask.
What is sentThe address, title and page text of links you save. Your notes only if you allow it. Never your other links’ notes or your account.
Limits500 links and 50 questions a month. You can turn it off any time and delete AI data.
