Home variants

Now: library first

The same first screen as every bookmark app: sidebar, toolbar, a wall of cards.

  • Familiar, nothing to learn
  • Hides everything that makes Shelfmark different
  • Looks like Raindrop, Pocket, mymind

Today

The first screen answers “what should I come back to?” before “what do I have?”. The library is one click away and still under the fold.

  • Shows the product: reminders, reading progress, auto-filing with Undo, a forgotten link
  • Sidebar: Today replaces Revisit; All links stays
  • Needs a few days of use to fill; first run shows the empty-library page

Today, editorial

Variant B with a voice: serif headings (Literata, already used in Reader), the brand sage as accent, and your note shown as a coloured label on the card.

  • Recognisable at a glance; nobody in the category does serif + notes on cards
  • Notes become the signature: “why I saved it” is visible
  • Sage is a change from the chosen Graphite; Graphite stays an option

Ask first

The first screen is one large field: search by words or meaning, or ask. Reading in progress sits under it.

  • Strongest AI story, very different from the category
  • Empty-looking for people who browse rather than search
  • Weak when AI is off: falls back to plain search
