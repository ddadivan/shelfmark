AiMark is the one sign that something was made by AI: a suggestion row, a summary, the AI settings. It is the `spark` icon in `text-muted`, nothing more.

**Angular:** `ui-icon name="spark"` with a `label` where the mark carries meaning alone ("Summary by AI", "Suggested by AI"); inside a labelled row ("Suggested") it is `aria-hidden`.

**Classes:** `sm-ai-mark` and `sm-ai-spark` (colour `ai`); `sm-ai-tile` (48px, 16px corners, the only AI gradient: `ai-bg` → `accent-bg`, spark 24px), used only at the top of the first-run intro.

**Tokens:** `ai` (light #4C54B5, dark #A9AEF2; 5.4:1 or more on `surface`, `bg` and `ai-bg`), `ai-line` (light #767ECB, dark #666CB8; 3:1 on `surface` and `bg`, for the dashed chip edge), `ai-bg` (light #E9EAF8, dark #23253D). Indigo, kept apart from the plum folder colour.

**Icon `spark`** (added to the set for #476): a four-point star with concave sides and a small plus at its lower right. Stroke source: `M11 4c.6 3.9 2.6 6 6.5 6.5-3.9.6-5.9 2.6-6.5 6.5-.6-3.9-2.6-5.9-6.5-6.5C8.4 10 10.4 7.9 11 4z M18.5 15v4 M16.5 17h4`; 1.75px line, round caps and joins, converted to filled outlines like every icon (Icons).

When it shows (disclosure rules, `docs/ai/FEATURES.md`):

- On everything AI produced that the user has not accepted: suggestion chips, summaries, search-by-meaning results and their "why it matched" line, Ask your shelf answers.
- Not on accepted things: an accepted tag or folder is the user's data and has no mark.
- On auto-applied things while they can be undone: "Moved by AI" with Undo in the toast; a badge "Moved by AI" with Undo on the Folder field and the spark inside auto-added tag chips in Edit link, until the user keeps or changes them.
- Generated text adds the quiet line "Generated by AI, may be inaccurate" (`ai.disclaimer`, 12px `text-muted`) under chat answers and under a summary where it has room; on cards the same words are the mark's tooltip and accessible name ("Summary generated by AI, may be inaccurate").

Style rules:

- One mark everywhere. No second "AI" glyph, no robot, no wand.
- Colour `ai` everywhere, so the mark stands out quietly from the graphite UI; the text next to it keeps `text` / `text-muted`. Never `accent`, never animated on its own; the only gradient is the intro tile.
- 14px next to 12–13px text, 16px in controls and settings, 18px in a settings tile, 32px in the empty-state tile (the largest size).
- The mark leaves when the content becomes the user's: an edited summary has no mark, an auto-applied folder or tag loses its «Moved by AI» mark once the user keeps or changes it.
