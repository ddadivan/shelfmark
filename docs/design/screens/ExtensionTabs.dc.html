2b · All tabs after Sort with AI

Sort with AI gives each ticked tab its own folder and tags: a third line under the address with the AI spark, the folder (its colour tile and name) and 1 to 2 tags. AI picks only your existing folders; a tag you do not have yet is dashed (new, created on save). While it works each row shows a grey placeholder line and the button says "Sorting N links…". Then the button goes away. Tap a row's folder to pick another one for that link; the Folder field now says "Picked by AI for each link". Each tab counts as one link of the monthly AI allowance; if it runs out, the rest keep the Folder field's folder and the quiet AI line says so. A tab ticked after sorting has no suggestions and takes the Folder field's folder.

Data and privacy

Switching to All tabs checks the listed tabs' addresses (without #fragment) against your library; nothing is saved until Save; only ticked tabs are saved or sent for AI. Titles stay in the browser. The privacy page and the store listing say so.
