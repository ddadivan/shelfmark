Shelfmark is a personal project that stores the links you save. This page explains what we keep and why, in plain words.

What we store

  • From Google or GitHub: your name, email address and avatar — only to sign you in.
  • Your links, titles, notes, folders and tags, and when you last opened a link.
  • Technical logs (time, request status) without the content of your links, kept for [N] days.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data and don't show ads.
  • We don't read your links ourselves; previews are fetched automatically.
