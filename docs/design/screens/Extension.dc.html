Motion and behaviour

The popup opens with the browser's own animation; inside it, states change with a 120 ms fade, and the check in "Saved" pops in once (the saving moment, scaled down: no card landing, there is no feed). The folder picker slides in from the right (200 ms ease-out) and back out (120 ms ease-in). Reduced motion: fades only. Light and dark follow the browser's colour scheme; the popup has no theme switch. Every text goes through translation keys, in the six app languages.
