Motion and behaviour

The popup opens with the browser's own animation. Inside it, states change with a 120 ms fade; the check in "Saved" pops in once (the saving moment, scaled down: there is no feed for a card to land in). The folder picker slides in from the right (200 ms ease-out) and back (120 ms ease-in). Signed out: the cards fly in and float (state 5). Illustrations play once. Reduced motion: fades only, cards and scenes at rest. Light and dark follow the browser's colour scheme; the popup has no theme switch. Every text goes through translation keys, in the six app languages.
