5 · Конспект без AI

A digest the person makes by hand: highlight a sentence → «Додати в конспект», write points in their own words, reorder, then keep it on the card, copy it as text or share a link (board 7). Works for everyone, AI on or off. The same editor shows AI-made items on board 6.

What this board covers

Selection toolbar, highlight and toast, the digest panel with points and quotes, writing a point, the item menu, the empty digest, and the phone versions.

Decisions shown (#539)

  • Without AI: highlight → «Додати в конспект»; the person writes points by hand.
  • Editor: points + quotes with paragraph links.
  • Actions: «Зберегти в картку», «Скопіювати текстом», «Поділитися посиланням».

Open questions for the author

  • One digest per link (shown) or several?
  • Highlights that are not in the digest: allow them (a separate «highlight» action) or keep highlight = quote in digest (shown)?
