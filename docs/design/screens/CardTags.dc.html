frontend-architecture-notes +3 ▢ Conference talks 2026 ☆
  • Popover: ui-popover, glass (@include glass, 18px corners, padding 10/12/12px, max 320px wide), heading “All tags” (caption, 600, text-muted), then every tag as a sage chip (wraps; a long one ends in “…”). role="dialog" named by its heading; non-modal.
  • Position: above “+N”, lined up with its start edge (flips to its end edge, then below when there is no room); it grows from the corner next to “+N” (enter(pop) / leave(pop); a fade under reduced motion).
  • Opens: hover or keyboard focus on “+N” after 150 ms; the pointer has 250 ms to travel into the panel, which keeps it open. A click or tap pins it open (touch never hovers); Enter or Space also moves focus to the first chip.
  • Closes: Esc (focus back on “+N”), a press outside, Tab past the last chip, a second click on “+N”, or choosing a tag.
  • A chip lists the links with that tag (All links, filtered). Read-only cards (shared collection) show plain chips.
