Anatomy and timing

Lead — the link’s preview with a check when a link was saved; the folder’s icon in its colour when something moved; a tinted icon for everything else (sage for done, red for delete and errors, amber for reminders).

Text — what happened on the first line, what it happened to on the second (title, folder, count). The second line truncates with “…”.

Timing — 3 s quick confirmations, 4–5 s status and saves, 8 s anything with Undo, with a countdown line along the bottom. Errors and reminders stay until handled. Hover, focus or a touch pauses the timer.

Motion — rises 18 px with a light spring (420 ms) and leaves with a 150 ms fade. With reduced motion it only fades.

Surface — frosted glass, 18 px corners, the floating shadow. Dark theme uses the same glass on graphite.

Access — role=status for everything except errors (role=alert). Undo is a real button and is reachable by Tab; ⌘Z also undoes while the toast is visible. Esc dismisses.
