Turn on AI in Shelfmark?

  • Suggests tags and a folderFrom the ones you already have. You decide: nothing is applied unless you tap it or choose Automatic in settings.
  • Writes short summariesOne or two sentences per link. Marked as made by AI and may be inaccurate; edit them any time.
  • What is sent to the modelA link’s address, title, description and text extracted from the page. Never your other links, your notes (unless you allow it) or anyone else’s data.

Details in the privacy policy. You can turn AI off, or one feature at a time, in Settings › AI.

Shown once per account; the same text stays in Settings › AI and the privacy page. The soft indigo-to-sage tile with the spark is the only gradient in the AI design; everywhere else the mark is a plain indigo icon.

3 · Desktop — next step: process the links saved before (hotfix 2.2.1)

AI is on. Process your 248 saved links?

Links you saved earlier haven’t been read by AI yet. It will write short summaries and suggest tags and folders, and search by meaning will find them like new ones.

  • Nothing changes without your choice: suggestions wait for you.
  • Runs in the background, usually a few minutes. Stop any time.
  • Doesn’t use the monthly limit: there is a separate allowance of 5,000 links.
