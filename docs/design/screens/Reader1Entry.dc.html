1 · Де починається читання

Every place a person can start reading an article. «Читати» opens the reading page (/read/:id); the copy is made on that first open, or earlier for «На потім» and favourites. Only the kinds article, document and post (#537) get it. Desktop light unless marked; one dark check in section 3.

What this board covers

Entry points to the reader and the «Є копія» state: card (rest, hover, menu), list row, Edit link, search, dead-link card, copy ahead for «На потім» and favourites, phone sheet.

Decisions shown (#539)

  • Reading needs no AI; it works on Free, demo and with AI off.
  • Copy on demand; ahead only for «На потім» (#396) and favourites.
  • Kinds: article, document, post; never video, shopping, code, app.
  • Dead link: «Оригінал недоступний», the card opens the copy.
  • Trash removes the copy with the link.

Open questions for the author

  • Shortcut R for «Читати» and Shift+Enter in search: OK, or another key?
  • Should a card of an article open the reader by default (instead of the original) once a copy exists? Shown: no, the card still opens the original.
  • Is «Є копія» worth showing on every card, or only in Edit link and on hover?
