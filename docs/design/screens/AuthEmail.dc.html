How the screens connect

All six use the Sign in layout: the frosted card in the middle, saved links floating around it. “Continue with email” on Sign in opens screen 1. Create account leads to Check your inbox; the email link signs you in. Forgot password sends a link to screen 5; an old or used link lands on screen 6. Floating cards drift slowly and stop with reduced motion; below 720 px they hide and the phone layout takes over.
