Some pages were written long before anyone used paragraph elements properly. Their text sits straight in a table cell, broken into lines with break elements, and the extractor still has to read it as an article instead of giving up on it.

This second run of text is the second paragraph of the page. It talks about the same old habits of the early web, when every layout was a table and every gap was a spacer image, and nobody worried about screen readers or phones.

A third run closes the page with a few more words about why such pages still matter: they are often the only record of a small community, a hobby or a piece of local history, and reading them comfortably is exactly what a reader mode is for.
